# Team avatar prompt

Use this prompt to generate consistent Meet the Team avatar illustrations.
Provide a reference photo only as a loose guide for facial structure and vibe.

```text
You are creating a square (1:1) stylized human avatar illustration for a SaaS product “Meet the Team” page.

The avatar must be consistent with a previously established team style:
modern, professional, human-centered, and privacy-conscious.

REFERENCE:
Use the provided photo only as a loose reference for general facial structure, age range, hair type, and overall vibe.
Do NOT aim for photorealistic likeness.

STYLE:
- Stylized 3D human illustration
- Semi-realistic, clearly illustrated (not photo-like)
- Adult, professional, modern SaaS aesthetic
- Not cartoonish, not toy-like, not exaggerated
- Similar in style to modern startup team avatars (calm, clean, confident)

FACE & EXPRESSION:
- Preserve general face shape and proportions, but simplify details
- Avoid exact recognizability
- Natural eye size (not oversized)
- Clean eyebrows, soft facial definition
- Light stubble or clean-shaven depending on reference
- Calm, confident, approachable expression
- Very subtle smile or neutral professional look
- No dramatic emotions

HAIR:
- Inspired by reference photo, but slightly refined and simplified
- Clean, intentional hairstyle
- No messy or extreme styling unless core to identity

POSE & FRAMING:
- Head and shoulders portrait
- Slight angle (not fully front-facing)
- Relaxed, upright posture
- Hands NOT visible
- No gestures touching face or neck

CLOTHING:
- Smart-casual SaaS style
- Examples: minimal hoodie, crewneck sweater, light jacket, simple blazer, or clean shirt
- No ties
- No formal suits
- No logos, no text, no patterns
- Muted, neutral colors (navy, blue, gray, beige, olive)
- Clothing should feel modern and tech-oriented, not corporate-heavy

MATERIAL & LIGHTING:
- Soft matte materials
- Clean, smooth surfaces
- Soft studio lighting
- No harsh shadows
- No cinematic or dramatic lighting
- Balanced contrast

BACKGROUND:
- Solid flat background ONLY
- Color: #E7EBEF (or project-defined light neutral)
- No gradients
- No textures
- No objects

COMPOSITION:
- Centered subject
- Square canvas (1:1)
- Clean edges
- High-resolution
- Designed specifically for use inside a profile card on a website

STRICTLY AVOID:
- Photorealism
- Childish or cute styles
- Big head / chibi proportions
- Clay toy, plush, knitted, or doll aesthetics
- Painterly, watercolor, or sketch styles
- Sunglasses or accessories hiding facial features
- Text, UI elements, frames, borders, or icons
- Background scenery or environmental context

FINAL GOAL:
The result should look like a real, intelligent, trustworthy team member,
while clearly being an illustration.
All team avatars must look like they belong to the same product, the same brand,
and the same design system.
```
